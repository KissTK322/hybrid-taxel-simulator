**Reading of item 1:** above percolation and after a first thermal conditioning cycle, sourced values cluster at **−0.06 to −0.15 %/K (NTC)**. Fresh / near-percolation / soft-matrix films are much larger (−0.5 to −2.8 %/K). Rigid-matrix low-loading composites can flip to PTC (+0.04 to +0.11 %/K) because matrix thermal expansion opens tunnelling gaps. No %/K value was found for CNT on TPU specifically.

**What this means for the 30 µm gap over 650 µm domes (derived, using only sourced CTEs):** free thermal growth of a dome is h·α·ΔT. For a PDMS dome (340 ppm/K): 650 µm × 340 × 10⁻⁶ = 0.22 µm/K, i.e. about 8.8 µm over a 40 K rise — roughly 29 % of a 30 µm gap, if the spacer that sets the gap is a low-CTE material (polyimide 20 ppm/K or copper 16.5 ppm/K expand ~20× less). If the spacer is the same elastomer as the dome, the gap instead scales with the gap height itself (30 µm × α × ΔT, negligible). For TPU the same formula applies but no numeric α was sourced; the BASF figure only shows it is of the order of 10⁻⁴ /K and rises with temperature.
